\begin{abstract}
R03 closed the $h\cdot h\cdot c$ $\sigma$-composition gap in reduced variables but left a residual defect for
$Y=c^*\cdot G(c,h)$: 13 of 33 images failed the ghost rows $ccK$ and $cc\bar c$. We derive $\bar\sigma(Y)$ from the
reduced master $\bar S=S_{EH}+\int\tilde K\cdot s_0h+\int c^*\cdot s_0c$ with $\tilde K=K+\bar c F'(h)$. The three
pieces are $X_K=(D^T\tilde K)\cdot G$, $X_C=\rho\,T(X_K)$ (the $\tilde K$ expansion, not an independent kinetic term),
and $X_S=-s_0h\cdot\delta Y/\delta h$ with $\epsilon_S=-1$. Nothing is fitted. The raw kinetic alternative
$\bar c\,\square\,G$ (Q08's handmade $X_C$) fails every generator. With the derived image, \NP{} of \NGens{} pass and
\NF{} fail, all on $ccK{+}cc\bar c$, and every failure carries a single hatted contraction on the $c^*$ index. No
completion of the three pieces removes them. The obstruction is recorded as OPEN. Excluding the failing images from
$\operatorname{im}\bar\sigma$ (they are not BRST images), one finds $\dim\bar H=\HB$ conditional classes; R03's 18 all
survive and two more appear. No direction is certified physical. Outcome: \textbf{Partial}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Derived $\bar\sigma$ on $Y=c^*\cdot G(c,h)$}
\subsection{Reduced master}
After integrating out $B$, the independent variables are $(h,c,\tilde K,c^*)$ with $\tilde K=K+\bar c F'(h)$. The
classical reduced action is
\[ \bar S = S_{EH}[h] + \int \tilde K\cdot s_0 h + \int c^*\cdot s_0 c . \]
There is no independent $\bar c$: it sits inside $\tilde K$. The linearized antibracket $\bar\sigma=(\bar S,\cdot)$ on a
local ghost-number $-1$ functional $Y$ is therefore fixed by functional derivatives of $\bar S$.

\subsection{The three pieces}
For $Y=\int c^*_\mu G^\mu(c,h)$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\displaystyle\frac{\delta\bar S}{\delta c}\cdot\frac{\delta Y}{\delta c^*}
      = \tilde K\cdot\frac{\delta(s_0h)}{\delta c}\cdot G =(D^T\tilde K)\cdot G$.
      At three points only the free part of $s_0h=Dc$ contributes, giving the $K$-sector amplitude $X_K$.
\item Expanding $\tilde K=K+\bar c F'(h)$ converts $X_K$ into a $(K,\bar c)$ pair. The ghost equation of the reduced
      formalism is exactly this expansion on amplitudes: $X_{\bar c}=\rho\,T(X_K)$ with $\rho=-2$ and $T$ the Q08 map.
      So $X_C=\rho\,T(X_K)$. This is \emph{not} an independent kinetic term $\bar c\,\square\,G$.
\item $-\dfrac{\delta\bar S}{\delta h^*}\cdot\dfrac{\delta Y}{\delta h} = -s_0h\cdot\delta Y/\delta h$, i.e.\ $h\to s_0h=Dc$
      on $G$, landing in the $c^*cc$ sector. The relative sign $\epsilon_S=-1$ is the antibracket's field/antifield sign
      (the same sign that fixes $\lambda_F=\lambda_P=-1$ in Q09); both signs were tested in R03, and $-1$ is uniquely better.
\end{enumerate}
The $c\to s_0c$ piece of the antibracket produces four-point amplitudes at this order and drops.

\subsection{What is ruled out}
\begin{itemize}
\item Q08's handmade $X_C=(-\tfrac12)p^2(c^*\to\bar c)$, and every other global multiple of $p^2(c^*\to\bar c)$: the
      joint nullspace of $(X_K,\,p^2(c^*\to\bar c),\,X_S)$ on all 33 generators is empty, and every tested $(a,b,c)$ fails
      all 33 on the reduced system.
\item Truncations of $X_S$ by the rule $s_0\hat h=0$ (drop $T_{a3}$ before polarising, or set $X_S=0$ when $G$ depends on
      $\hat h$): both raise the failure count from 13 to 19.
\item Any global reweighting of $(X_K,X_C{=}\rho T,X_S)$: empty nullspace (R03).
\end{itemize}
The derived image is therefore the unique candidate built from the reduced master with the standard channel operators.

\section{G-sector outcome}

The remaining \NF{} fail \emph{both} $ccK$ and $cc\bar c$ (never $hhc$ or Bose alone). Pattern: every failure has a
single hatted contraction on the $c^*$ index ($T_{\cdot\,gv}$ with only $H$ elsewhere). The six monomials that carry
$T_{\cdot\,gv}$ together with a second $T$ on the graviton side all pass. No completion of the three pieces removes
the thirteen. We record
\[ \textbf{OPEN: $G$-sector $T(c^*)$ obstruction.} \]
Whether a refined treatment of hatted indices in $D^T$ (or in the ghost-row assembly) would close them, or a genuine
obstruction in the continuum theory is present, is not settled here.
